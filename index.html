Trust & responsible use

Transparent about what the toolkit does — and does not do.

Academic judgement remains with the user

The toolkit supports research and publishing workflows but does not replace scholarly judgement, peer review, editorial decisions, or verification of references and journal requirements.

Public demos are evaluation versions

The current public applications are lightweight browser-based tools. Institutional access controls, analytics, authentication, and payment systems are not represented as existing features unless they are separately developed and agreed.

Abstract2Journal provides an evidence-oriented matching score, not an acceptance probability. Journal names, article metadata, indexing, aims & scope, APCs, and submission rules should always be verified on official sources before submission.

About the project

Built from real academic workflows.

Academic AI Toolkit brings together four independent micro-applications developed to address recurring tasks in research and publishing: prompt construction, reviewer-response structuring, keyword generation, and journal targeting. The commercial direction is institutional pilot first, followed by a tailored licence only where real demand is demonstrated.
