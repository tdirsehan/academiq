Why AcademIQ

Open, focused, and practical.

01

Focused

Low-friction

03

Open access

Everyone can use the same tools; donations are voluntary and never gate functionality.

Responsible use

AcademIQ Tools supports academic work but does not replace scholarly judgement, source verification, peer review, or editorial decisions.

Decision-support outputs

Prompt Quality, Title Fit, Journal Fit, statistical results, and related outputs are decision-support indicators — not guarantees of publication, causality, or methodological adequacy.
