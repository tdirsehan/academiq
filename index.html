About the project

Built from real academic workflows.

Academic AI Toolkit is a portfolio of lightweight research and publishing tools developed to make common academic tasks more structured, transparent, and reusable. The objective is not to replace researcher judgment, peer review, or publisher guidance, but to support better preparation and decision-making.
The current applications are demonstration-stage web tools. Researchers should verify references, journal policies, submission requirements, and AI-generated outputs before academic or publication use.
