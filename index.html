Responsible academic use

Decision support, not automatic academic judgement.

The toolkit is designed to support academic workflows. It does not replace scholarly judgement, source verification, peer review, editorial decisions, or institutional research governance.

Privacy and data handling

Several modules operate locally in the browser. Abstract2Journal uses OpenAlex to retrieve publication evidence. Users should avoid entering confidential or restricted information into public evaluation tools and should review each module’s own notes before institutional use.

Scores are decision-support indicators

Title Fit, Aims & Scope Fit, Cover Letter Quality, Prompt Quality, and Journal Fit are support scores, not guarantees of publication quality or journal acceptance. Journal Fit in particular is not an acceptance probability.

About the project

Practical academic AI, organised around real publishing tasks.

Academic AI Toolkit is built as a portfolio of focused browser-based applications rather than one general-purpose chatbot. Each module addresses a specific academic task, making the workflow easier to demonstrate, evaluate, teach, and discuss with institutions.

The commercial direction is intentionally institution-first: public demonstration, limited pilot, evidence gathering, and only then a tailored institutional decision.
