What makes a useful research question?

A question should be clear, answerable with evidence and feasible with available resources. Causal wording needs a design that supports causal inference. A literature review is required before claiming originality.

Scope before quantity. If your problem cannot support the requested number of distinct questions, the brief asks for clarification instead of filler.
